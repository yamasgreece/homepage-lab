The Walk

One neighbourhood, one appetite.

We start in the backstreets of Psyrri and end at the Varvakios central market — 3.5 hours on foot, 8 to 10 tastings, and never more than 10 guests. Small enough to talk, slow enough to taste, honest enough to remember.
